## Checkpoint measurements

- Firmware source [0bdf375](https://github.com/CodingSlapfu/SLAP-5/commit/0bdf37500ae1adfe39497d8e33b8f0912d6b076b). Candidate identity `FM-1_75000132`. SHA-256 `3ed039aaf62cd3a52c66881f8f35257036ff3a946072a3073abb82cb814bd1c3`.
- Native application 557,836 / 581,564 bytes. Static RAM 87,864 / 98,304 bytes. Pool 335,568 / 344,064 bytes, leaving 8,496 bytes. Application write range `[0x4000, 0x93000)`, with the dedicated recovery record sector. Loader simulation retained boot head, songs and settings.
- Inline-worker host measurements on the preceding graph commit `d2ae97d`, before the two patch-only corrections, measured P95/P99/peak of 159/234/765 µs at 112 BPM, 177/243/1071 µs at 137 BPM and 221/274/539 µs at 160 BPM. One trial per mode and tempo. The 160 BPM tail was higher than the simultaneous baseline. These CPU timings cannot establish FM-1 callback or Core 1 deadlines.

32 host fixtures passed. The separate dual-core suite passed worker, single-core and discarded-worker PCM parity. All nine 112/137/160 BPM EDIT transition scenarios matched their controls. Seven visualizers drew identical frames across four master levels. The 136-patch sweep confirms graph reachability and native-size rendering and leaves typography fit and hardware acceptance open.
