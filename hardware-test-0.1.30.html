SLAP-5 v0.1.30 FM-1 test checklist

Experimental installer · Previous 0.1.29 release

Back up your songs first. Use a disposable copy for saving and loading. V12 saves with active filters may not load in earlier firmware.

  1. Play a dense five-track loop at 112, 137 and 160 BPM. Listen for crackling and distortion. Enter and exit Sound Lab several times while the loop plays. Watch HOME for screen tearing, note tails and track highlights.
  2. In MIXER, sweep K3 from low-pass through neutral to high-pass. Do the same with K4 on each track. Check neutral sound, combined sweeps, and whether switching tracks recalls each setting. Verify MIXER SOLO and DUCK, then check HOME level, steps, pan and swing.
  3. Compare Sound Lab first-detent FM6 routing and Wavetable graphics. Stop playback on Dancer and confirm the choreography gives way to recovery poses. Test Beat Repeat on its third black key, Vinyl Stop on its tenth, and Rewind on its eleventh with both track and mix routing. Cycle through the other visualizers. Save and reload the disposable copy and check whether the filter settings return.
  4. Hold GLO five seconds for diagnostics. Move K3 to reset counters before a trial. Record the tempo, page or action, audible symptom, callback P95/P99/peak, deadline misses, XRUNs, and Core 1 status if available. GLO cycles through worker and DMA views. A short written summary is enough. One screenshot of an unusual reading is optional.

If playback becomes severely distorted or the unit repeatedly hangs, stop testing. Use the M-Flasher recovery instructions and the previous firmware or your own backup.
