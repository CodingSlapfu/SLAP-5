SLAP-5

Five-track groovebox firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1.

Open SLAP STUDIO Play the FM-1 emulator

Features

Five tracks

Four pitched tracks and a drum track with independent pattern lengths, pan, level and mute. Edit two notes per pitched step or work with six independent drum lanes. Set velocity, gate, hit probability and timing offset.

25 synth engines

Browse 128 factory patches. Play the FM, analog, modeled strings, keys, formants, bell and texture engines.

Nine effects pages

Shape each track with filter, slicer, inserts, reverb, tempo delay, chorus and drive.

Eight visualizers

Switch between audio-reactive scenes and a waterfall that reads the actual sequencer notes.

Manual

HOME
SELECT chooses a track. ALGO changes BPM. The four knobs set pan, steps, level and swing. Press HOME again to open visualizers.
SEQ
SELECT chooses a step. Press a key to add or clear its note. Use the four knobs to change note, velocity, gate and hit probability. Hold SEL and turn SELECT to choose the note or drum lane. Hold SEL and turn knob 4 to adjust timing offset.
EDIT / ENV / LFO
EDIT opens the patch browser. Play keys to audition. ENV sets attack, decay, sustain and release. LFO sets waveform, rate, phase and fade.
REC
Tap HOME REC to overdub during playback. From stopped, REC starts an audible four-click count-in. Tap again to end the take. Hold REC for two seconds to open settings for 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars. While stopped, ARP in RECORD undoes the previous take if its pattern has not changed.
Recording storage
Older banks remain readable. Long takes and sixteenth-step offsets use the new sparse format, which stores up to 587 events per bank. A bank that is too dense leaves the old saved bank intact. Studio JSON v3 supports extended length and timing.
SEL / FX
SEL opens harmony for the selected melodic track. FX opens the effect chain. SELECT chooses a track and ALGORITHM moves through the nine FX pages. The four numbered knobs edit the current effect.
GEN DRUMS
Select Track 5 and press SEQ to open its sequencer. Press SEQ again to enter GEN DRUMS. With transport stopped, adjust the four knobs and press SEL to generate. Press SEQ to return to the editable pattern.
ARP
Choose the mode, tempo division, octave range and gate. Hold notes on a melodic track to play them in sequence.
SAVE
Stop playback. SELECT chooses one of eight slots. Hold the blinking white key for four seconds to save. Press SEL to load its saved song.
MIDI
Send notes on channels 1–5 to address tracks 1–5. The FM-1 keyboard sends notes on the selected track’s channel.
