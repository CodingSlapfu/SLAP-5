SLAP-5

Five-track groovebox firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1.

Open SLAP STUDIO Play the FM-1 emulator

Features

Five tracks

Four pitched tracks and a drum track with independent pattern lengths, pan, level and mute. Edit four notes per pitched step or work with 16 independent drum lanes. Set velocity, gate, hit probability and timing offset.

25 synth engines

Browse 136 factory patches, including eight new saw programs. The original 128 patch IDs are preserved. Play the FM, analog, modeled strings, keys, formants, bell and texture engines.

Nine effects pages

Shape each track with filter, slicer, inserts, reverb, tempo delay, chorus and drive.

Seven visualizers

Follow each track in DNA Helix, Ferrofluid, Gravity Rings, Solar System, Digital VU, Slap Reactor and the Dancer.

Inside the instrument

SLAP-5 Sound Lab manual

SLAP STUDIO opens in Song Mode. Add or duplicate a scene, then select a track clip to edit it. Choose scene bars and repeats before playing the song. During playback, click a scene to queue it. Quick REC captures the active scene’s selected clip, and drum generation applies to the current scene. Add per-bar level and pan automation for each track. Export a song for all five tracks and every scene, or export the selected scene as a standalone Studio project.

The primary browser installer offers 0.1.18. See the experimental installer for the current hardware-testing release.

HOME
SELECT chooses a track. ALGO changes BPM. The four knobs set pan, steps, level and swing. Press HOME again to open visualizers.
SEQ
SELECT chooses a sixteenth-note step and previews its stored notes or drum hits. Sixteen cells make a bar. On melodic tracks, K1 sets pitch, K2 accent, K3 rest or play, and K4 hold length in steps. A zero hold uses the saved gate. Hold SEL to access hit probability, gate, fine hold and timing offset. Hold SEL and turn SELECT to choose the note or drum lane. SEL with EDIT copies, LFO cuts, ENV pastes, OCT− deletes and OCT+ cycles Normal, Ratchet and Fill.
EDIT / ENV / LFO
EDIT opens the selected engine graph directly. FM6 lets you choose a routing, then adjust the ratio and level of each of six operators. Turn SELECT inside FM6 Sound Lab to open operator envelopes. Hold OCT+ and turn K4 for feedback. WAVETABLE shows the scanning waveform and lets you adjust morph position and movement. Play keys to audition. ENV sets attack, decay, sustain and release. LFO sets waveform, rate, phase and fade.
REC
Tap REC on HOME to overdub. From stopped, REC arms for the first note or runs an audible four-click count-in, according to Settings. PLAY flashes green once the loop starts. Turn SELECT to move the active take to another track. Tap REC again to end the take. Hold REC for two seconds for recording options. On HOME, REC+OCT− undoes, REC+ARP cancels the take, and REC+OCT+ or REC+SEL twice clears the loop or selected track.
Recording storage
Older banks remain readable. FM6 ratio, level and wavetable settings use V10 banks. Per-operator envelopes and feedback use V11 banks. Long takes and sixteenth-step offsets use the new sparse format, which stores up to 587 events per bank. A bank that is too dense leaves the old saved bank intact. Studio JSON v3 supports extended length and timing.
MIXER
Hold EDIT for two seconds. K1 is level (0–100), K2 pan, K3 solo and K4 drum-triggered ducking on melodic tracks. White keys 1–5 mute tracks. FX PROC K2 adjusts the overall FX blend.
SETTINGS
Tap GLO. Under Audio, K3 toggles Auto Level and K4 toggles Auto Mix. Lights controls keyboard and button brightness. HOME shows battery bars, USB power, or a low-battery message. Hold GLO for five seconds to open diagnostics.
SEL / FX
SEL opens harmony for the selected melodic track. FX opens the effect chain. SELECT chooses a track and ALGORITHM moves through the nine FX pages. The four numbered knobs edit the current effect.
SLICER
On the Slicer FX page, K1 picks Off, Gate or Stutter. K2 selects a pattern, K3 selects the rate and K4 sets depth. Stutter blends a short captured repeat into the closed cells.
LIVE FX
Double-tap FX. Hold a black key to play an effect and tap SEL to switch selected track or full mix. Vinyl Stop slows the captured audio and drops its pitch while the sequencer continues.
GEN DRUMS
On Track 5, press SEQ twice. K1–K4 set style, hit density, bars and variation. Hold OCT+ while turning K3 for groove. Choose 1, 2, 3 or 4 bars (16, 32, 48 or 64 steps). Press SEL to generate and confirm replacement when prompted. During playback with a 16, 32 or 64-step drum loop, the pattern applies on the next bar. In the drum sequencer, SEL+GLO protects a step on its lane until another song is loaded. Use HOME REC+OCT− to undo.
DANCER
Press HOME again and turn SELECT to DANCER. K1 selects the dance pattern, K2 toggles the outline, K3 controls club lights and K4 toggles music reactivity.
ARP
Choose the mode, tempo division, octave range and gate. Hold notes on a melodic track to play them in sequence.
SAVE
Stop playback. SELECT chooses one of eight slots. Hold the blinking white key for four seconds to save. Press SEL to load its saved song.
MIDI
Send notes on channels 1–5 to address tracks 1–5. The FM-1 keyboard sends notes on the selected track’s channel.
